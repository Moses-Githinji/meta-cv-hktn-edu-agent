Dear Sir/Madam,

RE: REQUEST FOR ACCESS TO OFFICIAL KICD CURRICULUM DESIGNS

I write on behalf of ShuleWeb, a Kenyan company building Mada, an
AI-powered platform designed to make a teacher's work effortless when
translating the national curriculum into schemes of work, lesson plans,
and examination drafts.

Reason for writing

We are seeking your assistance in accessing KICD's official curriculum
design materials across all levels of the Competency-Based Curriculum
— Pre-Primary, Primary, Junior School, and Senior School. These designs
are currently published on the KICD website as embedded previews whose
downloading is disabled, which prevents our platform from retrieving
the official documents programmatically.

We therefore request that KICD grant our platform access to these
official curriculum designs — whether by enabling downloading on the
existing files, providing the documents directly, or advising on an
approved channel through which we may obtain them.

What this access would help us achieve

With access to the official designs, our platform would:

- Give teachers instant access to the official curriculum designs
  wherever they are, including in schools with limited connectivity,
  where the materials can be used offline once retrieved.
- Ensure the materials teachers work with are always accurate and up
  to date, because they would be drawn directly from KICD's official
  publications rather than from unofficial or outdated copies in
  circulation.

How this serves the teacher

Today, a teacher's time is divided between preparing the required
professional documents and doing the actual teaching. By placing the
official designs at the heart of our platform, Mada would relieve
teachers of the mechanical work of generating schemes of work, lesson
plans, and exam drafts from the curriculum — allowing them to shift
their full attention to teaching itself, confident that every document
they use is faithful to KICD's official designs.

We wish to assure you that the materials would be used strictly to
serve teachers in this manner, with full acknowledgement of KICD as the
source, and we are happy to discuss any terms, attribution requirements,
or technical arrangements you consider appropriate.

We look forward to your guidance and remain available for any further
information you may require.
