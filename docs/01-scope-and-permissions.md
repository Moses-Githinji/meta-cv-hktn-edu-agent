# Phase 1A — Scope and permissions

## Locked scope

- Learning areas: Grade 7 (Junior School) — Mathematics, Christian Religious
  Education, English, Kiswahili, Agriculture, French, one pack each under
  `packs/` with its curriculum file under `curriculum/`
- Curriculum: Competency-Based Curriculum (CBC), Kenya
- Coverage: one term only (Term 1 — see `teacher_context/term_calendar.json`)
- Source of truth: the official KICD Junior School Curriculum Designs for
  Grade 7 (Mathematics: First published 2022, Revised 2024; other subjects
  as listed on the KICD Grade Seven designs portal)
- Rule: outcome text is copied exactly from the official design. No paraphrase.
  Anything not yet transcribed is marked `TODO_TRANSCRIBE`, never invented.

Out of scope for Phase 1: other grades, Terms 2–3, exam papers,
KICD-approved course books.

## Official sources

1. KICD Grade Seven Curriculum Designs portal (Mathematics entry point):
   https://kicd.ac.ke/cbc-materials/curriculum-designs/grade-seven-designs/
2. KICD main site and contact: https://kicd.ac.ke — info@kicd.ac.ke — +254 20 3749900
3. Ministry of Education: https://www.education.go.ke
4. Kenya Education Cloud: https://kec.ac.ke — OER portal: https://oer.kec.ac.ke
5. Print authority: Junior School Curriculum Design, Mathematics, Grade 7,
   published and printed by the Kenya Institute of Curriculum Development.

Mirror PDFs (freeexams.co.ke, cbcelimu.com) are finding aids only.
The KICD design is the only citable source for outcome text.

## Roles

- Curriculum Owner: selects strands/sub-strands for the term, approves transcriptions.
- Teaching Agent Developer: builds `edu_kit/` and `teaching_agent/`, never edits outcome text.
- Teacher Reviewer: checks the term pack and timetable against classroom reality.
- Sign-off (Head of Department): approves the pack before classroom use.
- Rights Holder (KICD): grants permission to reuse outcome text.
